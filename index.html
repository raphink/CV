A point of view

IT is about people on both sides of the interface.

My work begins where implementation meets adoption. Guided by the Theory of Constraints, I track what limits a team’s flow—technical friction, unclear handoffs, or inaccessible knowledge—and make the path forward usable. The intervention changes with the problem: a UI, automation, a platform capability, or a learning experience. The goal is always the same: give people a clear way to understand the system, act with confidence, and improve it together.

01 · DEVOPS · 2005—NOW

Teach the why, not just the tool.

From Puppet and Terraform providers to Kubernetes and Cilium, I have taught the history, purpose, and trade-offs behind the tools—not just their commands. In the DevOps movement, the point has always been to improve human processes; automation without that understanding only moves the problem.

02 · PLATFORM ENGINEERING · 2012—NOW

Make the useful path easier than the workaround.

I turn complex infrastructure into practical models and tooling that help people see the system, make decisions, and operate it together. The recurring constraint: complexity without a shared view.

03 · TECHNICAL EDUCATION · 2008—NOW

Turn expertise into practice at scale.

From teaching colleagues at Orange how to package for Debian, to building programmes for Puppet, Docker, Terraform, Kubernetes, and cloud-native networking and security, I have made complex practice teachable. The latest generation—hands-on labs and the automation behind them—has supported more than 100,000 lab sessions. The recurring constraint: expertise trapped in a room.
